Chase is a journalist who has spent [his career](https://www.linkedin.com/in/chasejdavis/) working on challenging problems at the intersection of news, data and technology. He has led organizational change as a senior newsroom executive, managed teams of engineers as well as reporters, and has worked hands-on as a reporter, data journalist and developer in a variety of newsrooms.

He was an early leader in applying [machine learning](https://github.com/cjdd3b/fec-standardizer/wiki), natural language processing and other techniques from [data science and artificial intelligence](https://github.com/MinneapolisStarTribune/agate-ai) to newsgathering — an interest and skillset he continues to maintain both personally and professionally.

He is also a proud educator, having spent nearly a decade teaching data-driven and investigative journalism at the Missouri School of Journalism, Columbia University and the University of Houston.

In his spare time, he is an avid climber and endurance athlete. He lives in Minneapolis with his wife, Torey, and son, Jack.
